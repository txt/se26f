RULES

- Talks eat lecture time. Budget below is teaching minutes AFTER talks.
- Student talks cover some demanded topics (all.md); lectures do not
  duplicate what a scheduled talk covers -- lectures frame, talks detail.

TIME BUDGET (night = 4:30-7:15 = 165 min; minus ~15 min break/admin)
  no talks     : ~150 min teaching
  4-talk night : ~90 min teaching  (4 x 15 min talks)
  2-talk night : ~120 min teaching
  mid-term     : ~60 min teaching, AFTER the exam (fresh students sit the
                 exam; the lecture absorbs any exam overrun)
